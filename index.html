Mandatory Rules

📜 I. General Conduct

Respect the Narrative: This is a scripted environment. Intentionally trying to "break" the story or harass actors/staff ruins the experience for everyone.

No Toxicity or Harassment: We have zero tolerance for hate speech, discrimination, or targeted harassment. Keep the banter friendly.

No Self-Promotion: Do not advertise other servers, Discord links, or personal projects unless explicitly permitted in the #media channel.

Profile Standards: Ensure your nickname and profile picture are appropriate. No "invisible" names or offensive imagery.

⛏️ II. In-Game & Event Rules

Approved Mods Only: Only aesthetic mods (Optifine, Iris, Sodium) are allowed. Hacked clients, x-ray, or macro scripts are instant bans.

Exploit Reporting: If you find a "hole" in the script or a way to bypass a barrier, report it. Using it for an advantage is considered cheating.

Follow the Lead: When an Event Lead or Actor is speaking, cease all PVP and movement unless instructed otherwise.

Griefing Policy: Do not break blocks or items unless the script specifically calls for it. Most event maps are not "survival" worlds.

No Lag Machines: Any attempt to intentionally lag the server via entities, redstone loops, or packet spam will result in a permanent blacklist.

🎤 III. Voice Chat (VC) Etiquette

Push-to-Talk Recommended: To avoid background noise during cinematic moments, we highly suggest using Push-to-Talk.

Mute During Briefings: When a staff member is giving instructions, please mute your microphone.

No Soundboards or Ear-Rape: Loud, sudden noises or spamming soundboards will get you kicked from the VC and potentially the event.

Recording & Streaming: You are welcome to record/stream unless the event is marked as "SPOILER SENSITIVE." Check announcements before going live.

⚖️ IV. Enforcement & Appeals

Admin Discretion: Staff members have the final say in any dispute. Rules cannot cover every specific scenario; we expect you to use common sense.

Strike System:
• Warning: Minor infractions (spamming, accidental griefing).
• Kick: Repeated minor infractions or disruption of an active event.
• Ban: Cheating, severe toxicity, or intentional script sabotage.

Appeals: If you feel a decision was unfair, open a ticket in #support. Do not argue with staff in public channels.
